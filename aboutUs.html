About SRC Solutions

SRC Solutions is dedicated to providing fast, reliable, and friendly IT support for students and staff. Our helpdesk team specialises in resolving hardware issues, software problems, account access difficulties, and general technical queries.

Our mission is simple: keep your technology running smoothly so you can focus on what matters most — learning, teaching, and creating.

  • ✔ Software installation and support
  • ✔ Password resets and account recovery
  • ✔ Network and connectivity assistance
  • ✔ Device setup and configuration
  • ✔ Ticket-based support with real-time updates

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a support ticket?

Simply visit the “Submit a Ticket” page, fill out the form, and our team will respond as soon as possible.

How long does it take to receive a response?

Most tickets receive an initial response within 24 hours during normal operating hours.

Can I track my ticket progress?

Yes — log in to your account and visit the “My Tickets” page to view updates and status changes.

What should I include in my ticket?

Provide as much detail as possible: device type, error messages, screenshots, and steps to reproduce the issue.

Do you support personal devices?

Yes, we can assist with basic troubleshooting for personal laptops, phones, and tablets.
